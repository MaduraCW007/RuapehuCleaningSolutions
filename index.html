What We Do

Complete Cleaning Solutions

From a single bedroom to a full commercial complex — every service is delivered with the same uncompromising standard of excellence.

Regular Home Clean

Weekly or fortnightly cleaning tailored to your schedule. We handle dusting, vacuuming, mopping, bathrooms, and kitchen surfaces.

  • All rooms dusted & vacuumed
  • Bathroom & kitchen sanitisation
  • Floor mopping & surface polish
  • Bin emptying & tidying
From $89 / visit

Deep Clean

A thorough, top-to-bottom clean for move-ins, seasonal refreshes, or long-term rental handovers. No corner left untouched.

  • Inside oven, fridge & dishwasher
  • Skirting boards & window tracks
  • Inside cupboards & wardrobes
  • Grout & tile scrubbing
From $220 / clean

End-of-Tenancy

Maximise your bond return with our comprehensive move-out clean, trusted by landlords and property managers across the region.

  • Bond return guarantee
  • Certificate of completion
  • Landlord checklist compliant
  • Same-day availability
From $280 / property

Office Cleaning

Daily, weekly, or ad-hoc office cleaning that keeps your workspace professional, hygienic, and welcoming for staff and visitors.

  • Desks, screens & equipment
  • Kitchen & breakroom sanitation
  • Restroom deep clean
  • Reception & common areas
From $140 / visit

Hospitality & Accommodation

Specialist room turnovers, laundry management, and deep cleaning for lodges, motels, and Airbnb properties across the Ruapehu district.

  • Rapid room turnover
  • Linen change & laundry
  • Amenity restocking
  • Guest-ready presentation
From $65 / room

Window & Exterior Cleaning

Streak-free window cleaning inside and out, plus exterior pressure washing for driveways, decks, and building facades.

  • Streak-free guarantee
  • High-reach equipment
  • Frame & sill cleaning
  • Pressure washing available
From $120 / service
